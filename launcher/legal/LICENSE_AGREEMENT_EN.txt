Privacy Notice – Visual Launcher

Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short: the Launcher sends no data to the developer. There is no server of the Visual Client project, no telemetry, no analytics tools, no advertising and no automatic crash reports.

1. What is stored locally on your device

The Launcher stores the following in its user data folder:

- Account data: Minecraft name, UUID, skin address and the Microsoft refresh token. The token is protected with your operating system's encryption (Windows DPAPI, macOS Keychain, Linux Secret Service) where available.
- Settings: design, colours, background image, memory, window size, Discord settings, language.
- Game data: Minecraft, Fabric, Java, instances, mods and worlds.

Signing out, or deleting the data folder, removes this data completely.

2. What the Launcher connects to directly

The Launcher only connects to the following services, and only when you use the matching feature:

- Microsoft / Xbox Live (login.live.com, user.auth.xboxlive.com, xsts.auth.xboxlive.com) – sign-in
- Mojang / Minecraft services (api.minecraftservices.com, piston-meta.mojang.com, launchermeta.mojang.com, libraries.minecraft.net, resources.download.minecraft.net, textures.minecraft.net) – account check, game files, Java, skins
- Fabric (meta.fabricmc.net, maven.fabricmc.net) – mod loader
- Modrinth (api.modrinth.com, cdn.modrinth.com) – search and downloads
- The Discord app on your PC (local, only when "Discord status" is on) – the Launcher passes on to the Discord app what you play (instance, Minecraft version and, if enabled, the server IP). Discord shows this to your friends; Discord's privacy policy applies.

Through these connections the providers receive technically necessary data such as your IP address and, at sign-in, your account data. Their privacy policies apply, for example Microsoft's privacy statement. We have no access to this data.

3. Minecraft itself

When you start the game, Minecraft processes data under the rules of Mojang/Microsoft, for example for multiplayer, chat reports or the game's own telemetry. This is not part of the Launcher.

4. Your rights

Because we do not receive or store any personal data from you, there is nothing we could give you information about or delete. You manage your local data yourself. For data held by Microsoft or Modrinth, contact the respective provider.
