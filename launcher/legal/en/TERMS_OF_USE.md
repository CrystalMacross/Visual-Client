# Terms of Use – Visual Launcher

Last updated: 21 November 2026

These Terms of Use ("Rules of Use") set out how you may use Visual Launcher and the built-in Visual Client. They add to the Terms of Service.

## 1. Permitted use

You may use the Launcher privately and free of charge to:

- sign in with your own Microsoft account and play Minecraft: Java Edition,
- create instances with Fabric and Visual Client,
- install mods, resource packs, shaders and modpacks from Modrinth,
- customize the look of the Launcher, for example with your own hex colours or your own screenshots as the background.

## 2. Not permitted

You must not use the Launcher to:

- play Minecraft **without a valid license** or bypass license and account checks,
- sign in with **other people's accounts**, or use or pass on other people's credentials or session tokens,
- break the **Minecraft EULA**, the **Microsoft Services Agreement**, the **Minecraft Usage Guidelines** or the rules of servers you play on (for example with unauthorized cheats),
- redistribute third-party content against its license or bypass authors' download restrictions,
- overload the servers of Microsoft, Mojang, Modrinth or Fabric with automated mass requests,
- pass off modified versions of the Launcher as the official version or distribute them with malware.

## 3. Backgrounds and your own content

- Images you upload as a background stay **only on your device** and are not sent anywhere.
- Only use images you have the necessary rights to, and make sure yourself that they are appropriate for you.

## 4. Visual Client

- Visual Client is a purely client-side mod for visual changes and HUD elements.
- Each server decides whether mods are allowed. Check its rules before you join.

## 5. Consequences of breaches

If you break these Terms of Use, your right to use the Launcher ends. Bans by Microsoft, Mojang, servers or platforms are outside our control.

## 6. Contact and reports

You can report bugs and security issues on the issue page of the Visual Client GitHub repository. Never share tokens or passwords there.
